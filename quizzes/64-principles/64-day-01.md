## q1 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** Why is the sacred month of Kārtika celebrated as the foremost month of the entire year for spiritual practitioners?

**Options:**
- [ ] Vows observed during this month can only be undertaken by ascetics in forest hermitages
- [ ] Spiritual benefits are awarded only when accompanying rituals with massive golden donations
- [x] Lord Kṛṣṇa awards boundless spiritual merit for even the smallest loving offering of devotion
- [ ] Devotional practices bear fruit exclusively when aligned with specific planetary configurations

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 16.41-42

**Explanation:** Just as Lord Dāmodara is naturally affectionate toward His devotees, Kārtika is uniquely generous, awarding inexhaustible spiritual merit (*akṣaya-phala*) for even the simplest humble offering of love.

## q2 | multiple-select | easy

**Q:** Which of the following devotional practices are recognized as the five foundational limbs of the Kārtika vow (pañca-vratāni)? (Select all that apply)

**Options:**
- [x] Offering illuminated lamps of pure ghee before Lord Dāmodara (dīpa-dāna)
- [x] Taking a purifying holy bath before sunrise at the break of dawn (prātaḥ-snāna)
- [x] Daily watering, circumambulating, and reverently serving the sacred Tulasī plant (tulasī-sevana)
- [ ] Performing severe multi-week waterless fasts in solitary mountain caves

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 16.92-93

**Explanation:** The five core observances of Kārtika comprise early morning bathing (*prātaḥ-snāna*), offering lamps (*dīpa-dāna*), serving Tulasī (*tulasī-sevana*), keeping night vigils (*jāgaraṇa*), and formally concluding the vow (*udyāpana*).

## q3 | true-false | easy

**Q:** Rekindling or protecting another devotee's flickering lamp from being extinguished by wind awards immense spiritual merit and liberation.

**Options:**
- [x] True
- [ ] False

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 16.119-122

**Explanation:** Scripture emphasizes the boundless mercy of Kārtika by stating that even unintentionally reviving or shielding an offered lamp—as in the famous pastime of the mouse—awards liberation and spiritual elevation.

## q4 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** Why is the holy month of Kārtika also revered under the sacred name Ūrjā-māsa?

**Options:**
- [x] Because Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī is Ūrjeśvarī, the presiding queen of divine potency and Kārtika
- [ ] Because Mother Yaśodā exhibited her parental vigor while churning butter in Gokula
- [ ] Because King Bali offered his entire kingdom with tremendous administrative power
- [ ] Because the sun enters an energetic constellation that purifies the physical body

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 16.1 & 16.195-197

**Explanation:** In the Dig-darśinī commentary, Śrīla Sanātana Gosvāmī explains that *ūrja* represents the internal spiritual potency of pure devotion, of which Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī is the presiding deity (Ūrjeśvarī).

## q5 | multiple-select | easy

**Q:** Which sacred pilgrimage regions are celebrated for bestowing the most sublime spiritual results when observing Kārtika vows? (Select all that apply)

**Options:**
- [x] Mathurā-maṇḍala and the sacred groves of Vṛndāvana
- [x] The transcendental waters of Śrī Rādhā-kuṇḍa in Vraja
- [ ] Distant commercial trade capitals along the ocean coast
- [x] The purifying banks of the sacred Yamunā River

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 16.151-166 & 16.207

**Explanation:** Performing the Kārtika vow in Mathurā, Vṛndāvana, Rādhā-kuṇḍa, or along the Yamunā easily awakens pure love of God (*prema-bhakti*) and grants the Lord's eternal association.

## q6 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** What profound philosophical truth is revealed when Mother Yaśodā's ropes repeatedly fell short by two fingers while trying to bind Lord Kṛṣṇa?

**Options:**
- [ ] Ordinary household churning ropes lack the physical tensile strength to bind a child
- [x] Human endeavor must be united with the descending causeless mercy of the Supreme Lord
- [ ] Lord Kṛṣṇa was utilizing mystic illusion to escape maternal household discipline
- [ ] Mother Yaśodā needed to perform formal Vedic fire rituals before completing her task

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 16.200 commentary & Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.9.15-18

**Explanation:** The two-finger gap signifies that spiritual success requires two components: the devotee's sincere, persistent personal effort (*pariśrama*) and Lord Kṛṣṇa's compassionate grace (*kṛpā*).

## q7 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** Why is the eight-verse prayer Dāmodarāṣṭakam recommended for daily recitation before Lord Dāmodara throughout Kārtika?

**Options:**
- [ ] It details complex polemical debates to defeat rival intellectual schools
- [ ] It petitions the Lord for vast material opulence, health, and heavenly boons
- [ ] It serves as a formal legal atonement for accidental ritual mistakes
- [x] It possesses the unique transcendental potency to attract and please Lord Dāmodara

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 16.198 (Padma Purāṇa)

**Explanation:** The Dāmodarāṣṭakam spoken by Satyavrata Muni is celebrated as *dāmodarākarṣi*—a prayer that directly captivates the heart of Lord Dāmodara and grants pure devotional attachment.

## q8 | multiple-select | easy

**Q:** Which sacred festivals take place during the holy month of Kārtika? (Select all that apply)

**Options:**
- [x] Bahulāṣṭamī, the appearance day of Śrī Rādhā-kuṇḍa
- [x] Govardhana-pūjā and the grand offering of food (Annakūṭa)

**Explanation:** Kārtika is illuminated by numerous beloved festivals including Bahulāṣṭamī, Dīpāvalī, Govardhana-pūjā, Gopāṣṭamī, Prabodhinī Ekādaśī, and Kārtika Pūrṇimā.

## q9 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** What milestone in Lord Kṛṣṇa's childhood pastimes is commemorated on the festival of Gopāṣṭamī in Kārtika?

**Options:**
- [ ] Lord Kṛṣṇa receiving His first pair of wooden churning sticks in Gokula
- [ ] Lord Kṛṣṇa traveling to Mathurā to attend the wrestling match of King Kaṁsa
- [x] Lord Kṛṣṇa graduating from herding small calves to independently grazing the cows
- [ ] Lord Kṛṣṇa inaugurating the construction of the grand city of Dvārakā

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 16.284-286

**Explanation:** Gopāṣṭamī marks the joyful day when Nanda Mahārāja formally entrusted Lord Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma with the care of the full-grown cows, advancing from calf-herding (*vatsa-pāla*) to cow-herding (*go-pāla*).

## q10 | true-false | easy

**Q:** While pure cow ghee is the foremost offering for Kārtika lamps, offering lamps fueled with pure sesame oil is also celebrated as highly auspicious.

**Options:**
- [x] True
- [ ] False

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 16.103-106

**Explanation:** While cow's ghee (*gavya-ghṛta*) is recognized as supreme, pure sesame oil (*tila-taila*) is explicitly authorized and celebrated for lighting lamps before Lord Dāmodara.

## q11 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** What is the foundational purpose of cultivating Vaiṣṇava proper conduct (sad-ācāra) and observing devotional etiquette?

**Options:**
- [ ] To establish intellectual superiority over ordinary moralists in society
- [x] To bring supreme spiritual joy to the devotees and please the Supreme Lord
- [ ] To gain prestigious social titles and worldly administrative power
- [ ] To accumulate pious karma for promotion to the heavenly planets

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 1.1-3 & Dig-darśinī commentary

**Explanation:** The sacred practices of Vaiṣṇava etiquette (sad-ācāra) are compiled specifically to bestow transcendental bliss upon the devotees (*śrī-vaiṣṇavānāṁ pramude*) and to bring joy to the Supreme Lord.

## q12 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** How does sacred scripture evaluate even a fraction of a moment spent in the sincere association of the Lord's pure devotees?

**Options:**
- [ ] As equal to worldly prestige gained through lucrative state appointments
- [ ] As equivalent to temporary mystic powers attained through haṭha-yoga
- [ ] As an ordinary social interaction meant for psychological relaxation
- [x] As far superior to residency in heavenly planets or even impersonal liberation

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.287 (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.18.13)

**Explanation:** Sages declare that even a single moment (*lava*) of devotee association cannot be compared to the attainment of heavenly realms or even impersonal liberation (*mokṣa*), what to speak of fleeting worldly blessings.

## q13 | multiple-select | easy

**Q:** Which of the following interpersonal exchanges nourish and deepen spiritual friendship among Vaiṣṇavas? (Select all that apply)

**Options:**
- [x] Giving devotional gifts in charity and graciously accepting what is offered in return
- [ ] Competing aggressively with peers to secure prominent management positions
- [x] Inquiring about sacred spiritual truths and sharing confidential realizations of the heart
- [x] Honoring the Lord's sanctified food remnants (mahā-prasāda) together in joyful fellowship

**Ref:** Śrī Upadeśāmṛta (The Nectar of Instruction), Text 4

**Explanation:** In *The Nectar of Instruction* (Text 4), Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī reveals the six loving exchanges (*ṣaḍ-vidhaṁ prīti-lakṣaṇam*) that nourish devotee relationships: giving and receiving gifts, sharing and inquiring into confidential thoughts, and offering and honoring mahā-prasāda together.

## q14 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** What natural emotional response should arise in a practitioner's heart whenever a faithful devotee of the Lord comes into view?

**Options:**
- [x] Spontaneous gladness and genuine warmth in the heart
- [ ] Critical scrutiny of the devotee's physical appearance
- [ ] Proud indifference while waiting for the devotee to bow first
- [ ] Envious calculation of the devotee's public reputation

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.315 (Skanda Purāṇa)

**Explanation:** Scripture warns that failing to experience genuine joy upon seeing a devotee (*yāti no harṣaṁ darśane*) leads to spiritual decline; greeting devotees with a joyful heart is essential Vaiṣṇava etiquette.

## q15 | multiple-select | easy

**Q:** What is the proper devotional mindset and etiquette when receiving and honoring mahā-prasāda? (Select all that apply)

**Options:**
- [x] Receiving the sanctified food with reverence in the humble mood of a servant
- [x] Distributing and sharing the preparations joyfully among all devotees present

**Explanation:** Mahā-prasāda should be honored without worldly discrimination, received in the mood of a humble servant (*dāsyena*), shared generously with fellow devotees, and taken while remembering great bhaktas.

## q17 | true-false | easy

**Q:** A true Vaiṣṇava is characterized by forbearance, responding with kindness, respect, and peaceful words even when confronted by harsh or insulting speech.

**Options:**
- [x] True
- [ ] False

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.18-19, 10.221-222

**Explanation:** Saintly devotees are naturally patient and tolerant (*titikṣavaḥ kāruṇikāḥ*); even when pierced by harsh words, they respond with peaceful composure, respect, and unconditional well-wishing.

## q18 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** How should a practicing devotee view the outward social origin or caste background of a sincere Vaiṣṇava?

**Options:**
- [ ] As a valid basis for establishing temporary administrative rank in prayer halls
- [ ] As an important factor determining whether one may accept their association
- [ ] As a necessary consideration when distributing sacred food preparations
- [x] As completely irrelevant to their exalted spiritual status as an eternal servant of God

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.165-167 (Itihāsa-samuccaya & Padma Purāṇa)

**Explanation:** Scripture strictly warns against evaluating a devotee by their material lineage or bodily origin (*vaiṣṇave jāti-buddhiḥ*), affirming that a sincere devotee of the Lord is always pure and worshipable.

## q19 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** How is a person regarded who performs elaborate daily rituals for the Supreme Lord while persistently neglecting and disrespecting the Lord's devotees?

**Options:**
- [ ] As an accomplished philosopher who has transcended social dealings
- [ ] As a strict orthodox ritualist whose exclusive focus pleases the Lord
- [ ] As an exalted renunciate who need not associate with others
- [x] As a proud pretender whose worship is insincere and deceptive

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.365

**Explanation:** One who claims devotion to the Supreme Lord while failing to honor His devotees (*tadīyān*) is condemned as a proud pretender (*dāmbhika*), for the Lord never accepts worship from someone who disdains His servants.

## q20 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** If an unintentional offense or misunderstanding occurs between devotees, what is the proper path of rectification?

**Options:**
- [ ] Offering monetary donations to local civic institutions while avoiding the devotee
- [ ] Remaining silent and aloof in order to protect one's social prestige and honor
- [x] Approaching the devotee with sincere humility, begging forgiveness, and pleasing their heart
- [ ] Performing rigorous physical penances in complete isolation without reconciliation

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.316-318 & Dig-darśinī commentary

**Explanation:** The Lord is never satisfied when a devotee's heart is hurt; the only genuine remedy is to approach the aggrieved devotee with heartfelt humility, beg for forgiveness, and restore loving harmony.
