## q1 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** Among all spiritual practices and forms of worship in the universe, which activity is celebrated as the highest and most pleasing to the Supreme Lord?

**Options:**
- [ ] Constructing towering golden palaces for prominent earthly rulers
- [x] Rendering loving worship and service to the devotees of Lord Viṣṇu
- [ ] Performing silent ascetic penances on remote snowy mountain summits
- [ ] Mastering complex grammatical commentaries on speculative philosophy

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.337-338 (Padma Purāṇa)

**Explanation:** While direct worship of Lord Viṣṇu is sublime, scripture reveals that rendering loving service to the Lord's faithful devotees (*tadīyānāṁ samarcanam*) is the topmost practice and brings Him the greatest joy.

## q2 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** How does sacred scripture evaluate even a fraction of a moment spent in the sincere association of the Lord's pure devotees?

**Options:**
- [ ] As equal to worldly prestige gained through lucrative state appointments
- [ ] As equivalent to temporary mystic powers attained through haṭha-yoga
- [x] As far superior to residency in heavenly planets or even impersonal liberation
- [ ] As an ordinary social interaction meant for psychological relaxation

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.287 (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.18.13)

**Explanation:** Sages declare that even a single moment (*lava*) of devotee association cannot be compared to the attainment of heavenly realms or even impersonal liberation (*mokṣa*), what to speak of fleeting worldly blessings.

## q3 | true-false | easy

**Q:** When two Vaiṣṇavas meet and offer humble prostrated obeisances (daṇḍavat-praṇāma) to one another, Lord Viṣṇu personally manifests between them in great joy.

**Options:**
- [x] True
- [ ] False

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.323 (Tejo-draviṇa-pañcarātra)

**Explanation:** The Pañcarātra scriptures explain that whenever two devotees humbly bow down to each other on the ground, the Supreme Lord personally stands between them holding His divine weapons.

## q4 | multiple-select | easy

**Q:** Which of the following interpersonal exchanges nourish and deepen spiritual friendship among Vaiṣṇavas? (Select all that apply)

**Options:**
- [x] Giving devotional gifts in charity and graciously accepting what is offered in return
- [ ] Competing aggressively with peers to secure prominent management positions
- [x] Inquiring about sacred spiritual truths and sharing confidential realizations of the heart
- [x] Honoring the Lord's sanctified food remnants (mahā-prasāda) together in joyful fellowship

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.124-128, 10.476-479 & Upadeśāmṛta 4

**Explanation:** Devotee relationships flourish through six affectionate exchanges (*ṣaḍ-vidhaṁ prīti-lakṣaṇam*): giving and receiving gifts, sharing and hearing confidential thoughts, and offering and partaking of mahā-prasāda together.

## q5 | who-said-this | easy

**Quote:** "My dear Goddess, of all varieties of worship, the worship of Lord Viṣṇu is supreme. But even higher than the worship of Lord Viṣṇu is the loving service of His devotees."

**Context:** Spoken in the Padma Purāṇa during an intimate dialogue on the ultimate perfection of spiritual worship.

**Q:** Who spoke this celebrated verse establishing the supremacy of worshipping devotees?

**Options:**
- [ ] Lord Brahmā to Sage Nārada
- [ ] Sage Vasiṣṭha to King Dilīpa
- [x] Lord Śiva to Goddess Pārvatī
- [ ] Śukadeva Gosvāmī to King Parīkṣit

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.337 (Padma Purāṇa)

**Explanation:** Lord Śiva personally instructs Goddess Pārvatī that worshipping the servants and associates of Lord Viṣṇu (*tadīyānāṁ samarcanam*) is the topmost of all spiritual endeavors.

## q6 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** What natural emotional response should arise in a practitioner's heart whenever a faithful devotee of the Lord comes into view?

**Options:**
- [x] Spontaneous gladness and genuine warmth in the heart
- [ ] Critical scrutiny of the devotee's physical appearance
- [ ] Proud indifference while waiting for the devotee to bow first
- [ ] Envious calculation of the devotee's public reputation

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.315 (Skanda Purāṇa)

**Explanation:** Scripture warns that failing to experience genuine joy upon seeing a devotee (*yāti no harṣaṁ darśane*) leads to spiritual decline; greeting devotees with a joyful heart is essential Vaiṣṇava etiquette.

## q7 | multiple-select | easy

**Q:** What is the proper devotional mindset and etiquette when receiving and honoring mahā-prasāda? (Select all that apply)

**Options:**
- [x] Receiving the sanctified food with reverence in the humble mood of a servant
- [x] Distributing and sharing the preparations joyfully among all devotees present
- [ ] Demanding the finest and largest portions before serving senior practitioners
- [x] Remembering and offering heartfelt respects to exalted devotees of the Lord

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 8.218-220 & 9.414

**Explanation:** Mahā-prasāda should be honored without worldly discrimination, received in the mood of a humble servant (*dāsyena*), shared generously with fellow devotees, and taken while remembering great bhaktas.

## q8 | missing-link | easy

**Chain:**
- Feeling more humble than a blade of grass (tṛṇād api sunīcena)
- Being more tolerant than a tree (taror api sahiṣṇunā)
- ?
- Always ready to offer all respect to others (mānadena)

**Q:** What essential virtue completes this foundational crest-jewel of Vaiṣṇava character?

**Options:**
- [ ] Demanding that peers recognize one's seniority
- [ ] Harboring subtle pride in personal service achievements
- [x] Remaining devoid of desire for personal prestige (amāninā)
- [ ] Seeking public acclaim for charitable activities

**Ref:** Śrī Śikṣāṣṭaka 3 & Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 11.415-416

**Explanation:** Lord Caitanya’s famous third verse reveals the four essential ornaments of a devotee: humility (*tṛṇād api sunīcena*), tolerance (*taror api sahiṣṇunā*), freedom from false prestige (*amāninā*), and offering respect to everyone (*mānadena*).

## q9 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** How should an advancing devotee relate to fellow practicing devotees on the spiritual path?

**Options:**
- [ ] With competitive rivalry to achieve greater recognition
- [ ] With cautious detachment to prevent personal interaction
- [ ] With judgmental correction of their occasional slip-ups
- [x] With sincere friendship (maitrī) and mutual spiritual encouragement

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.75 (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.2.46)

**Explanation:** A practicing Vaiṣṇava (*madhyama-bhāgavata*) is distinguished by offering genuine, heartwarming friendship (*maitrī*) to all devotees of the Lord.

## q10 | true-false | easy

**Q:** A true Vaiṣṇava is characterized by forbearance, responding with kindness, respect, and peaceful words even when confronted by harsh or insulting speech.

**Options:**
- [x] True
- [ ] False

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.18-19, 10.221-222

**Explanation:** Saintly devotees are naturally patient and tolerant (*titikṣavaḥ kāruṇikāḥ*); even when pierced by harsh words, they respond with peaceful composure, respect, and unconditional well-wishing.

## q11 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** How should a practicing devotee view the outward social origin or caste background of a sincere Vaiṣṇava?

**Options:**
- [ ] As a valid basis for establishing temporary administrative rank in prayer halls
- [ ] As an important factor determining whether one may accept their association
- [ ] As a necessary consideration when distributing sacred food preparations
- [x] As completely irrelevant to their exalted spiritual status as an eternal servant of God

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.165-167 (Itihāsa-samuccaya & Padma Purāṇa)

**Explanation:** Scripture strictly warns against evaluating a devotee by their material lineage or bodily origin (*vaiṣṇave jāti-buddhiḥ*), affirming that a sincere devotee of the Lord is always pure and worshipable.

## q12 | multiple-select | easy

**Q:** Which of the following behaviors must be strictly avoided in the sacred assembly of devotees and before the Deity? (Select all that apply)

**Options:**
- [ ] Chanting the holy names attentively on japa beads
- [x] Boasting of one's own talents, wealth, or personal virtues
- [x] Speaking loudly, gossiping about mundane affairs, or quarreling
- [x] Criticizing and finding fault with other devotees (para-nindā)

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 8.449-453

**Explanation:** Devotional etiquette warns against thirty-two offenses, which include criticizing others (*para-nindā*), praising oneself (*nija-stotram*), shouting, and arguing in the presence of the Deity and devotees.

## q13 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** How is a person regarded who performs elaborate daily rituals for the Supreme Lord while persistently neglecting and disrespecting the Lord's devotees?

**Options:**
- [ ] As an accomplished philosopher who has transcended social dealings
- [ ] As a strict orthodox ritualist whose exclusive focus pleases the Lord
- [ ] As an exalted renunciate who need not associate with others
- [x] As a proud pretender whose worship is insincere and deceptive

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.365

**Explanation:** One who claims devotion to the Supreme Lord while failing to honor His devotees (*tadīyān*) is condemned as a proud pretender (*dāmbhika*), for the Lord never accepts worship from someone who disdains His servants.

## q14 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** What is the primary purpose of discussing devotional duties and spiritual topics among sincere practitioners?

**Options:**
- [x] To nourish and expand mutual transcendental love and affection (anyonya-prīti)
- [ ] To exhibit personal intellectual dominance over newer devotees
- [ ] To compile a record of philosophical mistakes made by companions
- [ ] To replace the necessity of performing one's daily personal chanting

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.479

**Explanation:** Exchanging spiritual realizations and inquiring into devotional duties is practiced specifically to cultivate heartfelt love and joy among devotees (*anyonya-prīti-vṛddhaye*).

## q15 | single-choice | easy

**Q:** If an unintentional offense or misunderstanding occurs between devotees, what is the proper path of rectification?

**Options:**
- [ ] Offering monetary donations to local civic institutions while avoiding the devotee
- [ ] Remaining silent and aloof in order to protect one's social prestige and honor
- [x] Approaching the devotee with sincere humility, begging forgiveness, and pleasing their heart
- [ ] Performing rigorous physical penances in complete isolation without reconciliation

**Ref:** Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 10.316-318 & Dig-darśinī commentary

**Explanation:** The Lord is never satisfied when a devotee's heart is hurt; the only genuine remedy is to approach the aggrieved devotee with heartfelt humility, beg for forgiveness, and restore loving harmony.
